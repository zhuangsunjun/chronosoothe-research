# Safety Boundaries

ChronoSoothe is an educational wellness and research-oriented software framework. It is not a medical device, diagnostic tool, treatment tool, or substitute for qualified professional care.

## Consumer Output Allowed

The consumer-facing product may provide:

- gentle acupressure
- point-location education
- breath pacing
- body awareness prompts
- stop conditions
- safety cautions
- professional-care escalation
- source transparency
- optional subjective feedback tracking

## Consumer Output Not Allowed

The consumer-facing product must not provide:

- needle insertion instructions
- needle depth or angle
- reinforcing/reducing manipulation
- moxibustion, heat, or burning instructions
- bloodletting guidance
- herbal formula instructions
- disease diagnosis
- disease treatment claims
- emergency-care substitution
- claims of proven clinical efficacy

## Stop Conditions

Users should stop immediately if they experience:

- pain
- numbness
- dizziness
- faintness
- distress
- skin irritation
- unusual symptoms
- worsening symptoms

## Seek Professional Care

Users should seek qualified medical care for serious, severe, unexplained, or rapidly worsening symptoms, including:

- chest pain
- trouble breathing
- stroke-like symptoms
- fainting
- major injury
- severe pain
- active infection
- unexplained numbness or weakness

## Higher-Risk Situations

Extra caution is required for:

- pregnancy
- bleeding disorders
- anticoagulant use
- implanted medical devices
- active wounds, swelling, fractures, or skin damage near a point
- serious chronic disease
- children, frail older adults, or medically complex users

ChronoSoothe should route these cases toward professional guidance rather than stronger self-care instructions.

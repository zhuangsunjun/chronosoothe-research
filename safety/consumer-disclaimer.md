# Consumer Disclaimer

ChronoSoothe is an educational wellness prototype. It does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure disease.

ChronoSoothe does not provide acupuncture, needling, moxibustion, bloodletting, herbal treatment, or medical advice. Any point guidance is limited to gentle acupressure and point-location education.

Use only light, comfortable pressure. Stop immediately if you feel pain, numbness, dizziness, distress, skin irritation, or anything unusual.

Seek qualified medical care for serious, severe, unexplained, or rapidly worsening symptoms. Do not use ChronoSoothe as a substitute for professional diagnosis or treatment.

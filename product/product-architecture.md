# Product Architecture

ChronoSoothe should feel simple for consumers and rigorous for reviewers.

## Consumer Flow

1. Confirm time and optional location.
2. Show one primary point.
3. Show point image and plain-language location.
4. Guide a short gentle press-and-breathe ritual.
5. Show stop conditions.
6. Collect optional subjective feedback.
7. Hide advanced rule detail behind an expandable explanation.

## Advanced Flow

Advanced users and reviewers may inspect:

- local mean solar time
- day and hour ganzhi
- Ling Gui Ba Fa residue
- Fei Teng / Bagua cross-check
- Zi Wu Liu Zhu channel-time layer
- Na Zi / Na Jia support layer
- Five Phase and Bagua metadata
- source status
- authority status
- safety restrictions

## Lineage Intake Flow

Methods that cannot safely auto-generate consumer prescriptions should be placed in a separate lineage-intake area. These may ask for additional parameters or remain locked until reviewed.

Examples:

- Master Tung
- Five Element acupuncture
- Huangdi Neizhen
- Tian Yi Shen Zhen
- complete Zhu-style spacetime acupuncture treatment sequencing

## UI Rule

The main page should answer one question:

> What gentle point can I press now, and how do I do it safely?

Everything else is secondary.

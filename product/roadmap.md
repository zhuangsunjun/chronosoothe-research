# Roadmap

## Phase 1: Public Research Package

- Publish public README.
- Publish v1.1 whitepaper draft.
- Publish safety boundaries.
- Publish source-audit workbook.
- Publish public method notes.
- Publish product architecture.

## Phase 2: Expert Review

- Recruit 1-3 licensed acupuncture or East Asian medicine reviewers.
- Review Ling Gui Ba Fa mappings and test vectors.
- Review Na Zi / Na Jia support labeling.
- Review Tai Yi / Tian Xing boundaries.
- Review Zhu-style spacetime architecture framing.
- Record reviewer decisions in the source-audit workbook.

## Phase 3: Usability Pilot

- Run 5-user private pilot.
- Measure point-location confidence.
- Measure instruction clarity.
- Measure safety-boundary comprehension.
- Collect confusion terms.
- Revise app copy and whitepaper language.

## Phase 4: Public Beta

- Publish consumer disclaimer.
- Publish clear no-claims page.
- Add feedback form.
- Keep advanced rule layers folded by default.
- Monitor confusion, discomfort reports, and misuse risk.

## Phase 5: Research Archive

- Revise whitepaper after expert review.
- Archive a citable version on Zenodo or OSF.
- Consider academic preprint only after references, pilot framing, and advisor review are stronger.

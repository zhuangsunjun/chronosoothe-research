# ChronoSoothe Research

ChronoSoothe is a source-audited spatio-temporal acupressure framework for safe, non-invasive self-care.

It explores how classical East Asian timing systems, such as Ling Gui Ba Fa, Fei Teng Ba Fa, Zi Wu Liu Zhu, Na Zi, Na Jia, Five Shu point theory, Eight Vessel confluence points, ganzhi cycles, Five Phase correspondences, and Bagua mappings, can be digitized into an explainable consumer wellness interface.

ChronoSoothe is not a diagnostic system, treatment system, clinical decision tool, or acupuncture prescription engine. The consumer-facing scope is limited to gentle acupressure, breath pacing, point-location education, stop conditions, and professional-care escalation.

## Why This Exists

Most wellness products treat recommendations as static. Classical East Asian medicine preserves time-aware frameworks that relate clock time, seasonal rhythm, stems and branches, channels, points, and body-space correspondences.

ChronoSoothe asks a narrow product and research question:

> Can source-audited classical timing rules be translated into a safe, explainable, non-invasive self-care ritual without making medical claims?

## Safety Position

ChronoSoothe does not provide:

- needling guidance
- moxibustion guidance
- bloodletting guidance
- disease diagnosis
- disease treatment claims
- emergency-care guidance
- clinical efficacy claims

For serious, severe, unexplained, or rapidly worsening symptoms, users should seek qualified medical care.
